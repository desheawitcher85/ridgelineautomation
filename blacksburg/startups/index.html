// The Blacksburg startup context

Technical Team.
Manual Ops.
That's the
Gap We Fill.

The VT corridor has produced a genuine cluster of technical companies — spinoffs, SaaS businesses, research commercializations, and consulting firms that are building real technology. The founding teams are engineers, researchers, and domain experts. Ops automation is something they know they should do.

The reason it doesn't happen internally is straightforward: the same engineers who could build the automation are the ones building the product. Pulling them off product work to build internal tooling is a trade most technical founders won't make — and shouldn't have to.

We come in as the ops automation partner. We scope it, build it, and run it. You keep full ownership of the systems we build. We don't create lock-in — we create documented, maintainable workflows that your team can take over at any point if you choose to bring it in-house.

For teams with AI in the product: we can incorporate LLM calls into automation workflows — lead qualification by Claude, document summarization by GPT-4o, customer communication drafting with model-in-the-loop review. We build this thoughtfully, with fallback logic for when the model misbehaves.

Most common first automation
Customer onboarding flow. It's usually the first place a growing startup feels the manual ops pain — and it's one of the cleanest to automate because the steps are well-defined.
Highest leverage automation
Lead qualification and routing. Cutting response time from hours to minutes on inbound leads directly impacts conversion rate. Most Blacksburg teams see measurable pipeline impact within 30 days.
For VT research spinoffs
Grant reporting, partner communication, and IP licensing workflows are all highly automatable. Research teams shouldn't be spending hours compiling progress reports.
Stack compatibility
HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, Linear, Jira, Slack, Stripe, GitHub, Airtable, Postgres, custom REST APIs — we've connected all of these. If it has an API, we can integrate it.

// Common questions

Blacksburg
Founders
Usually Ask:

We have a developer on staff. Why wouldn't they just build this?+
They could. The real question is whether their time is worth more on your product or on internal ops tooling. Most technical founders answer that quickly. We also specialize in automation tooling specifically — we build and maintain these workflows constantly, which means faster delivery and fewer edge cases than someone building their first automation between product sprints.
Can you build automation that uses AI/LLMs in the workflow?+
Yes. We build workflows with LLM steps for lead qualification, document analysis, customer communication drafting, and data extraction. We build with fallback logic — if the model returns something unexpected, the workflow handles it gracefully instead of silently failing. We're careful about where AI steps belong in a workflow and where deterministic logic is more appropriate.
We're pre-revenue and cash-constrained. Is there a minimal engagement?+
Start with the discovery audit. 50, 90 minutes, you get a written roadmap. If the highest-ROI automation is a ,500 build with a 60-day payback, the math is usually clear enough. We also offer milestone-based payment on builds for early-stage companies when it makes sense — ask in the discovery call.
Our data is sensitive. How do you handle security?+
We scope data security requirements in the discovery audit. For sensitive data, we favor self-hosted solutions (n8n self-hosted on your infrastructure), minimize data in transit, and avoid third-party platforms that require data residency compromises. We work under NDA and can build to SOC 2 or HIPAA-adjacent requirements when needed.
