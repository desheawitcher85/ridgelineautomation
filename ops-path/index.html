// What implementation actually looks like

3 Meetings.
~4 Hours Of Your Time.

Most automation projects fail not because the technology doesn't work, but because the internal champion gets buried in implementation. Endless calls, documentation requests, test scenarios, revision cycles. We've built our process specifically to prevent that.

You are the subject matter expert, not the project manager. Your job is to tell us how the work actually gets done — not to manage a build. We handle the build, the testing, the handoff documentation, and the monitoring after go-live.

Between meetings, you're not waiting on us — we're waiting on you to confirm when you're ready for the next step. The timeline moves at your pace.

// Meeting 1 — ~90 min
Discovery
Walk us through the current process exactly as it happens. We ask questions, document the workflow, and identify where automation fits and where it doesn't. You describe — we design.
// Meeting 2 — ~60 min
Review & Sign-Off
We show you the automation working in staging. You confirm it matches how the process should actually run, flag anything that needs adjustment. One round of revisions included — no extra charges for getting it right.
// Meeting 3 — ~30 min
Go-Live & Handoff
We walk your team through what was built, hand over the documentation, and confirm everything is running live. After this, we monitor and we're on call — you're not managing anything.
// Ongoing
We Watch It
We get the error alerts before you do. If something breaks — a downstream app changes its API, a field moves — we fix it before it affects your team. Retainer or included service window, depending on your package.

// What we actually build with

The Right Tool
For Your Stack.

We're not married to one platform. We use whichever tool fits your existing systems, your IT requirements, and the complexity of the workflow. Here's what's in the toolkit — and why each one exists.

// Enterprise workflows
n8n
Self-hosted option — runs on your infrastructure, not ours. Preferred when data sensitivity, compliance, or IT policy means you can't use a cloud-based platform. Full control over where data lives.
// Multi-app orchestration
Make
Visual workflow builder that handles complex, branching logic across dozens of apps. Better for high-volume workflows that need to route, transform, and route data across multiple systems simultaneously.
// Lightweight automation
Zapier
Right-sized for simpler, high-reliability automations your team can monitor without technical help. Best when the workflow is linear, the apps are mainstream, and IT simplicity matters more than customization.
// When the others won't reach
Custom
Custom API connections, webhooks, and scripted integrations for proprietary systems, legacy software, or anything that doesn't have an off-the-shelf connector. We document it like it was built by your in-house team.

We recommend the tool that fits your environment — not the one that's easiest for us to build.
