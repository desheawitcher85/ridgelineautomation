// Common questions

Before You
Book the Call.

Is it really free? What's the catch?+
It's free. No catch. We invest 30 minutes because it's the most efficient way for us to determine whether we can actually help you — and if we can, what the right solution looks like. We'd rather have a real conversation than send you a 12-page PDF proposal that doesn't fit your situation. If you're not a good fit, we'll tell you and we both move on.
Who will I actually be speaking with?+
DeShea Witcher — founder of Ridgeline Automation. Not a sales rep. Not a junior account manager. The person on the call is the person who would scope, build, and maintain your automation. That's intentional. You get more useful information from the person doing the work than from someone reading a discovery script.
Do I need to prepare anything before the call?+
Nothing formal. The most useful thing is to have a rough sense of the 2–3 tasks your team does most repeatedly — the stuff that happens every day or every week. But even if you come in unprepared, we'll find the opportunities through the conversation. That's what the call is for.
Is the call in person or on the phone?+
Typically phone or video call. For businesses in the Roanoke Valley who prefer to meet in person, we can arrange that — especially if it would help us understand your operation better. In-person is actually preferred for the paid discovery audit that follows if you decide to move forward.
What happens after the call if I want to move forward?+
If we're a good fit, the next step is the paid Workflow Discovery Audit — a 90-minute on-site session that maps every workflow in detail and delivers a written roadmap with ROI estimates. The audit fee is $750 flat, credited in full toward any build you proceed with. We'll explain this on the call so you know exactly what you're deciding.
How soon can I book a call?+
Submit the form and we'll respond within one business day with available slots. We typically have openings within 3–5 business days. If your situation is time-sensitive, mention that in the form and we'll prioritize.
