// The story

Why I
Built
This.

I've spent my career building growth systems — pipeline generation for global brands, paid media frameworks that turned losing accounts into 5x return engines, AI-driven operations for enterprise teams. The throughline in all of it: find the manual process, automate it, measure the result.

What I kept noticing was a gap. The tools I used for large organizations weren't making it to the businesses I drove past every day in Roanoke — the HVAC shop on Williamson Road, the accounting firm downtown, the medical practice on Electric Road running scheduling off a paper calendar and a prayer. Not because those businesses couldn't benefit. Because nobody local was offering it to them.

The vendors serving this market are remote. They don't know the difference between Roanoke City and Roanoke County, have never heard of Carilion, and they deliver a Zap, collect a check, and disappear. Ridgeline is the version of this that should exist here — built and maintained by someone with the actual technical background to do it right, a local phone number, and a reason to care whether it keeps working.

Fifteen years in Roanoke. These are my neighbors. I'm done watching them leave efficiency on the table because the right partner never showed up. That's the whole pitch. No fluff.
