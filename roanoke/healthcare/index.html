// Common questions

Healthcare
Practices
Usually Ask:

Are you HIPAA compliant? Can you sign a BAA?+
We are not a covered entity or business associate in the traditional sense — we're an automation builder. We build using platforms that offer BAAs (Make, certain Zapier tiers, n8n self-hosted) and we design workflows to minimize PHI in transit. We'll scope your specific compliance requirements in the discovery audit and recommend the right architecture for your risk tolerance.
We're on Epic. Can you integrate with it?+
Epic has a developer API (MyChart, FHIR R4) but access depends on your Carilion/Epic instance configuration. In many cases we work alongside Epic rather than directly into it — automating the communication and coordination layers that sit outside the EHR. We assess what's possible in the discovery audit.
Our front desk staff is resistant to change. How do you handle that?+
We design automations that reduce staff workload, not replace staff judgment. The framing that works: "you'll stop doing the repetitive stuff so you can focus on patients." We train your team on what the automation does and what they still control. Adoption resistance drops fast when staff realizes the automation is doing the tasks they hate most.
What's the ROI on appointment reminder automation specifically?+
It's one of the fastest-payback automations in healthcare. A 4-provider Roanoke practice seeing 120 patients/week with a 6% no-show rate loses roughly 7 slots/week.
